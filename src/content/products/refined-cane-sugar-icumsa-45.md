### Commodity Overview
**Brazilian ICUMSA 45 Cane Sugar** represents the pinnacle of sparkling white refined cane sugar, extracted from premium non-GMO sugarcane grown in the state of São Paulo, Brazil. Refined via continuous vacuum crystallization and carbonation, it offers exceptional solubility, sparkling clarity, and guaranteed food-industry purity.

### Chemical & Physical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification Standard | Method / Assay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Polarization** | Min 99.80% degrees | ICUMSA GS2/3-1 |
| **Attenuation Index** | Max 45 ICUMSA Units (RBU) | ICUMSA Method 4-1978 |
| **Moisture (Loss on Drying)** | Max 0.04% | Vacuum Oven (105°C) |
| **Ash Content (Conductivity)** | Max 0.04% | ICUMSA GS2/3-17 |
| **Solubility** | 100% Dry and Free Flowing | Visual & Filtration |

| **Sediments** | None detected | Physical Filtration |

### Shipping, Logistics & Inspection Protocol
- **Containerized Freight (FCL):** 25 MT net weight per 20ft container (500 bags of 50kg net). Shrink-wrapped and secured with heavy-duty polyester lashings.
- **Bulk Vessel Charters:** Supramax / Handymax dry-bulk vessels loaded at dedicated sugar berths in Santos terminals (CLI / Copersucar terminals) with average load rates of 8,000 - 12,000 MT/day.
- **Third-Party Surveyors:** SGS or Bureau Veritas appointed on all shipments to issue formal Certificates of Weight, Quality, Packaging, and Non-Radioactivity prior to bill of lading issuance.
