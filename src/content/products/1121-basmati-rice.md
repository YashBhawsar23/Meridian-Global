### Commodity Overview
Cultivated in the mineral-rich Himalayan river basins of Punjab and Haryana, our **1121 Extra Long Grain Basmati Rice** represents India's most prestigious culinary export. Naturally aged for a minimum of 24 months to enhance aroma and cooking elongation, each batch is milled in automated Sortex optical cleaning lines to eliminate chalky, damaged, or discolored grains.
