### Commodity Overview
Grown in the renowned onion belt of Nashik and Mahuva (Maharashtra & Gujarat), our **Fresh Indian Red Onions** are globally appreciated for their rich crimson color, firm texture, high solid content, and intense pungency. Harvested at optimal maturity and cured in shaded farm facilities, each onion possesses tightly bound outer skins that resist transit bruising and fungal development.

### Physical & Biological Quality Standards

| Characteristic | Specification Limit | Inspection Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Size Diameter** | 45mm - 60mm (Medium) / 55mm - 75mm (Jumbo) | Mechanical Ring Grader |
| **Skin Integrity** | Tight, multi-layered dry outer skin | Physical Inspection |
| **Rotten / Soft Bulbs** | ≤ 0.5% max | Manual Hand Sorting |
| **Doubles / Malformed** | ≤ 1.0% max | Pre-Packing Sorter |
| **Moisture (Curing)** | Optimum 78% - 82% internal moisture | Lab Assay |
| **Pesticide Residue** | Compliant with EU, GCC, and ASEAN MRLs | GC-MS / LC-MS Screening |

### Cold Chain & Container Stowage
- **Reefer Container Settings:** Loaded in 40ft High-Cube refrigerated containers maintained at +0°C to +2°C with 65% to 70% relative humidity and 25 CBM/hr continuous fresh-air de-humidification ventilation.
- **Port Transit Lines:** Fast-transit direct lines from Nhava Sheva (JNPT) to Jebel Ali (3–4 days), Colombo (3 days), Dammam (6 days), Singapore (7 days), and Rotterdam (16 days).
