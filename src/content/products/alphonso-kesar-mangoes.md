### Commodity Overview
Celebrated worldwide as the "King of Mangoes," our **Indian Alphonso (Ratnagiri / Devgad)** and **Gir Kesar (Gujarat)** mangoes are handpicked at physiological maturity from certified coastal and foothill orchards. Known for their fiberless golden pulp, rich saffron aroma, and intense sweetness exceeding 18° to 22° Brix.

### Phytosanitary & Quarantine Protocols
- **Hot Water Treatment (HWT):** 48°C for 60 minutes in automated APEDA-approved treatment facilities to eliminate fruit flies and anthracnose spores.
- **Vapor Heat Treatment (VHT) & Irradiation:** Certified radiation processing (400 Gy minimum dose) performed in BARC-accredited facilities for direct import into the United States, Japan, and Australia.
