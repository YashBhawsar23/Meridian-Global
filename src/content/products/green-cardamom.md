### Commodity Overview
Our export-grade **Guatemalan Green Cardamom** is harvested in the mist-shrouded highlands of Alta Verapaz. Recognized globally for its intense aroma, rich green pigmentation, and extraordinary volatile oil density, our pods undergo rigorous multi-stage mechanical and optical color sorting prior to packaging.
